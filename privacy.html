Privacy policy

This policy describes what Night Floor stores, what its ads involve, and what you can remove. Your balance and game history stay on your device. Purchases involve Apple, and optional rewarded ads involve Google, as described below.

What stays on your device

Your balance, your history of rounds, your statistics, and your settings are kept on this device. The app has no account, and nothing you do is sent to a server run by its makers. iCloud sync is not enabled.

Shared tables

To play with someone nearby, the app uses the local network. Devices in the same room find each other and exchange the moves of the table, such as bets and cards. That traffic stays on the local network and is not sent to the makers.

The first time you open a shared table, iOS asks for permission to use the local network. You can change that in Settings at any time.

Purchases

When you buy points, Apple processes the payment and tells the app which purchase was made. The app keeps a record of the points you received. It does not receive your payment details.

Ads

Some ads offer points in return for watching them. Those ads are served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads. Google's handling of that data is described in Google's privacy policy.

The app requests ads under settings that reduce personalization: non-personalized ad treatment, Google's under-age ad treatment (ads are tagged for users under the age of consent, as described in Google's ad targeting documentation), and a general maximum content rating. Non-personalized does not mean that no data is collected. Google may still collect and use data to serve and measure the ads themselves.

Because every ad request is tagged for users under the age of consent, the app currently does not show Google's consent form and does not ask for ad personalization choices; ads are served with the under-age protections described above. If Google's setup for the app ever requires a privacy-options entry point, the app retains one in the points store and you can revisit your choices there. The app does not request permission for cross-app tracking. See Google's GDPR guidance for AdMob for how these settings work.

Children

The app's policy asks that people under 13 do not use it. The app has no accounts, so there is no account data to remove; a parent or guardian can remove its locally stored points and history by deleting the app.

Apple calculates the App Store age rating from its content questionnaire. Frequent simulated gambling receives an 18+ rating under Apple's current rating rules. Follow the rating shown on the store listing for your region when the app is available. The app's own minimum age does not lower that rating.

Your choices

Deleting the app removes its balance and history from the device. The developer keeps no server copy and provides no balance recovery service or iCloud sync. Device backups are managed by Apple and your device settings. Deleting the app does not delete records held by Apple for purchases or by Google for ads; their privacy policies describe those choices.

If the device cannot save points, the app shows a warning and asks you to keep it open while it retries. If both the ledger and recovery journal cannot be written and the app is then terminated, an unsaved payout can be lost.
