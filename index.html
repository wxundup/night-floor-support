Support

Night Floor is a social casino for iPhone and iPad. Every game is played with points. Points have no cash value. There is no cash-out, and points cannot be withdrawn, sold, transferred, or exchanged for anything.

How the floor works

There are eight tables: Coin Flip, Dice Roll, Twenty-One, Crash, Pop It, Reels, Prize Wheel and Lucky Draw. Two of them open each day and the other six stay locked. The pair changes at midnight UTC, and every player sees the same pair worldwide. The floor shows a countdown to the next change.

Each table explains how to play. House Rules inside the app explain payout rounding, shared pots, and the published return ceiling. Results are random. Nothing about your balance, streak, or history changes an outcome.

Free points

The app adds up to 100 free points per UTC day. Free top-ups stop once your balance reaches 200. Days you miss are credited when you come back, still up to the 200 limit, and days spent sitting at the limit are not banked for later. Winnings, ad rewards, and purchased points can carry your balance above 200.

Rewarded ads

Watching an ad is optional and pays 25 points. You can do it at most three times per UTC day. The offer needs an internet connection and hides itself when it cannot be taken. Ads are served by Google; see the privacy policy for what that involves and for your ad privacy choices.

Point packs

The four point packs contain 100, 300, 1,000, or 3,000 points. When purchases are available, the app and Apple's purchase sheet show the price in your local currency. Apple handles the payment.

Packs are consumables saved on this device. They cannot be restored through the App Store or StoreKit once the app is deleted, and deleting the app removes its points and history. A purchase buys points only. It does not buy a result, a better chance, or a return on your points.

Playing with people nearby

Casual Co-Op lets nearby devices on the same local network play Hold'em or Party Blackjack together, each with its own points. The first time you open a shared table, iOS asks for permission to use the local network. Both devices need to be on the same network.

Where your stuff lives

Your balance, history, statistics, and settings are saved on this device only. The app has no account and no server, and iCloud sync is not enabled. Deleting the app removes its local data. The app has no balance recovery service. If you are having a problem, contact the address below before you delete the app.
